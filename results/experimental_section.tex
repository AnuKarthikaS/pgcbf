\section{Numerical Validation}
\label{sec:numerics}

\subsection{Benchmark, implementation and protocol}
The benchmark is the class-(H) telegraph reaction--diffusion--advection lattice
\begin{align*}
\dot q &= p,\\
\dot p &= -(\nu L_h + \alpha I)q - \gamma p - c\,W_f D_h W_f\, q\\
&\quad - \kappa(q - \sin q) + Bu + B_w w,
\end{align*}
on \(N_m = 160\) nodes (\(N = 320\) states) with Dirichlet data, \(\nu = 0.05\), \(\alpha = 1\),
\(\gamma = 1\), \(\kappa = 0.05\), two actuators (\(m = 2\)) and one disturbance channel. Here \(D_h\) is the
skew central difference and the advection filter is
\[
W_f = \Phi_f \operatorname{diag}(\underbrace{1,\dots,1}_{2},\,w,\dots,w)\,\Phi_f^\top ,
\]
where \(\Phi_f\) holds the first \(K_f = 4\) mass-normalised sine modes. The plain projector
\(\Pi_f = \Phi_f\Phi_f^\top\) is the special case \(w = 1\). The cutoff-mode weight \(w\) is a disclosed benchmark
parameter: it is a configuration literal, it is swept in Experiment 9, and the deployed value
\(w^\star = 2^{-11/2} \approx 0.0221\) is the one selected by that sweep. The ROM keeps \(K_m = 2\)
eigenmodes (\(K = 4\)), so \(K_f > K_m\) and the advection couples the ROM to the tail.

The advection is not folded into the damped-wave operator. \(A_{\mathrm{full}} = (J_{\mathrm{full}} - R_{\mathrm{full}})P_{\mathrm{full}}\)
stays the pure damped wave, so \(Q = \gamma P\) holds (relative error \(7.039\times10^{-15}\)), \(\lambda_{K+1} = 0.5\)
is unchanged, and (H2a) holds by eigenmode invariance (Lemma~\ref{lem:h2a_char}, residual \(6.348\times10^{-13}\)). For
the same reason the block \(P_0^{-1}\Phi^\top P_{\mathrm{full}}A_{\mathrm{full}}P_\perp\) vanishes to round-off
(\(1.27\times10^{-12}\)). The linear cross-coupling sits in the nonlinearity,
\(F_{\mathrm{nl}}(x) = A_{\mathrm{adv}}x + F_{\sin}(x)\) with
\[
A_{\mathrm{adv}} = \begin{bmatrix}0&0\\-c\,W_fD_hW_f&0\end{bmatrix},
\]
and its ROM--tail block \(A_{\mathrm{adv},r,\perp} := P_0^{-1}\Phi^\top P_{\mathrm{full}}A_{\mathrm{adv}}P_\perp\) of
Lemma~\ref{lem:delta_cert_classH} has norm \(\|A_{\mathrm{adv},r,\perp}\|_2 = 0.106\) in R2 and
\(0.005301\) in R1.

Two regimes are used. R2 has \(c = 1\) (\(\mathrm{Pe} = 20\)) and is open-loop unstable. R1 has
\(c = 0.05\) (\(\mathrm{Pe} = 1\)). The sampling period of R2 is \(T_s = 3\times10^{-4}\) with
\(\tau_A = 1.5\times10^{-5}\), \(\tau_B = 3\times10^{-5}\). This is a trade-off forced by the active tail coupling: at the
previous \(T_s = 5\times10^{-4}\) the certificate refuses at \(w^\star\), because
\(\delta_{\mathrm{total}} = 0.01821 > \delta_{\mathrm{cert}} = 0.01455\) and
the authority condition (H6), \eqref{eq:control_authority}, fails (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_refusals}). R1 keeps \(T_s = 0.002\).
The short period is set by the hold term, not by the plant time scale: the slowest mode (\(\omega_{\min} = 1.222\))
is sampled \(1.714\times10^{4}\) times per period \(2\pi/\omega_{\min}\) in R2 (\(2571\) in R1),
and the hold term \(\phi L_{\mathrm{hold}}T_s\) of \eqref{eq:delta_total_exact}, with \(L_{\mathrm{hold}} = 44.04\)
and \(\mathrm{Lip}(L_fh) = 1.77\), is a share \(0.4872\) of \(\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\) in R2
(\(0.5596\) in R1, where \(L_{\mathrm{hold}} = 1.474\) and \(\mathrm{Lip}(L_fh) = 0.7948\)).

Every constant of Sections~\ref{sec:problem}--\ref{sec:theory} is computed at run time in float64 by a function that
names the equation it implements. A pytest AST scan fails if a float literal appears in the numerical modules, and a
perturbation test fails if any certificate quantity does not depend on the configuration inputs. The certificate raises
(refuses) unless (H0)--(H7), the timing order and the codesign window all hold. This single flag is the
Theorem~\ref{thm:main_cbf} hypothesis row of Table~\ref{tab:exp1_hypotheses}. (H-dz) is checked separately and only
for Proposition~\ref{prop:deadzone_stability}. The Path-A LUT is indexed in port space \(v = g_r^\top P z\) by default.
When \(\mathcal C_{r,0} = \{\|z\|_P^2 \le E_{\mathrm{safe}}\}\) as in \eqref{eq:Cr0}, its range is
\(D_v = 2\sqrt{E_{\mathrm{safe}}}\max_j \sqrt{(g_r^\top P g_r)_{jj}} = 0.4472\) by \eqref{eq:Dv_closed}; the largest
sampled value is \(0.4472\) (both to four significant digits).
The modulus \(\mathrm{Lip}(L_fh)\) that enters \(L_{\mathrm{hold}}\) carries the factor of two of the gradient
\(\nabla_z(z^\top P z) = 2Pz\); it is cross-checked against finite-difference quotients of \(L_fh\) at random pairs in
\(\mathcal C_{r,0}\), which must not exceed the certified modulus.

The full-order plant is integrated with an exponential integrator (ETD2RK in the lattice eigenbasis). The latch
instants \(\tau_A\), \(\tau_B\) are integration nodes, so the switching times are exact. The disturbance is the worst
case of Remark~\ref{rem:hji_game} with \(\epsilon_{\mathrm{adv}} = 0.002\). All seeds come from the configuration
file. The suite runs 248 experiment assertions and 65 pytest tests on two CPU cores in
\(642.9\)~s, with a peak resident memory of \(1193\)~MB (Table~\ref{tab:runtimes}).

\subsection{Experiment 9: the cutoff-mode weight and the certifiable frontier}
The weight \(w\) is swept over 17 geometric values from \(1\) down to \(0.003906\) at the R2 P\'eclet number
(Table~\ref{tab:exp9_wsweep}, Fig.~\ref{fig:exp9}). At every \(w\) the open loop leaves \(\mathcal C\) on all seeds, so
the plant is unsafe throughout the sweep. For \(w \ge 0.08839\), the tail tolerance (H4) fails together with (H6) and
(H7). For \(0.03125 \le w \le 0.0625\), only the authority condition (H6) fails. The certificate accepts
for \(w \le w^\star = 0.0221\). Below \(w^\star\) the single-rate ablation (c) is no longer refused (first at
\(w = 0.01562\)). The deployed \(w^\star\) is therefore the largest grid value that is accepted with a positive
authority margin \(\delta_{\mathrm{cert}}-\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\) in \eqref{eq:control_authority} while every ablation still breaks. \(\|A_{\mathrm{adv},r,\perp}\|_2\) is proportional to
\(w\), and the tail share of \(\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\) falls from \(0.9968\) at \(w = 1\) to
\(0.2704\) at \(w^\star\).

For the plain projector (\(w = 1\)), the P\'eclet scan of Table~\ref{tab:exp9_plain_peclet} shows that the certifiable
advection speeds (\(c \le 0.02\), \(\mathrm{Pe} \le 0.4\)) and the open-loop-unstable speeds
(\(c \ge 0.5\), \(\mathrm{Pe} \ge 10\)) are disjoint. Under the Euclidean-ODD supremum that
defines \(M_\perp\) in~\eqref{eq:M_perp}, full-strength cutoff-mode advection is not certifiable at any P\'eclet number
where the open loop is unstable: no softening. The plain-projector result is the \(L^2\)-ODD conservatism of
Remark~\ref{rem:rda_cutoff}. Keeping \(A_{\mathrm{adv}}\) inside \(F_{\mathrm{nl}}\) preserves (H2a) exactly (eigenmode
invariance, Lemma~\ref{lem:h2a_char}) at the price of charging \(A_{\mathrm{adv},r,\perp}\) in \(M_\perp\) and
\(L_{\perp,P}\). The weight \(w\) attenuates the cutoff modes, which is what an \(H^1\)-bounded ODD would do for a smooth
field; certifying \(w = 1\) at \(\mathrm{Pe} \ge 10\) would require that \(H^1\) formulation,
which is outside the present theorem.

\subsection{Experiment 4: Theorem~\ref{thm:main_cbf} (positive case)}
The full method runs with the worst-case adversary on 16 seeds per regime from two kinds of initial
condition: the boundary start \(\tilde h_r(z(0)) = 0\), with the tail at its largest admissible value (H7), and the
interior start \(h_r(z(0)) = 0.5\,h_{\mathrm{act}}\). Conclusions (a)--(c) hold at every integrator

\subsection{Experiment 4b: budget utilisation along the certified trajectories}
Experiment 4b re-runs the closed loops of Experiment 4 and evaluates, at each of the 1600032 (R2) and
240000 (R1) integrator nodes over both starts and all seeds, every term of~\eqref{eq:ode_diff_ineq} next to
the bound the proof uses for it (Table~\ref{tab:exp4b_budget}). Each fraction below is the realised term over its
bound: the maximum over all nodes and, in parentheses, the median over the 102636 (R2) and 20238 (R1)
nodes whose sample \(z_k\) lies in \(\mathcal C_{r,\mathrm{act}}\), where the budget enters the proof. A fraction
0.9359 (R2) and 0.9157 (R1) of the nodes belong to periods with \(z_k \in \mathcal B_{\mathrm{int}}\);
there the proof uses only the Lipschitz branch, and its inequality holds at every such node.
\begin{itemize}
\item Adversary: \(0.8772\) (\(0.1454\)) in R2 and \(0.9761\) (\(0.2814\))
in R1. The worst-case \(w\) has norm \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{adv}}\) at every node, so the gap to one is the supremum
of \(\|d_r^\top\nabla\tilde h_r\|_2\) over \(\Crz\) in \(\overline d_h\).
\item Quantization and lag: \(0.3729\) (\(0.01712\)) and \(0.3672\)
(\(0.03416\)). The mismatch \(e_u\) reaches at most \(0.5872\) (R2) and \(0.5705\) (R1)
of its phase bound, so \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{CROWN}}\), \(\varepsilon_A\) and \(\Delta u_{\mathrm{lag}}\) are the conservative constants of this term.
\item Tail coupling: \(0.3161\) (\(0.003444\)) and \(0.2606\) (\(0.01455\)).
Of the factors of \(L_h\Lperp\epsP\), the two least used are the Lipschitz constant \(\Lperp\) (median
\(\|\Delta_\perp\|_2/(\Lperp\|x_\perp\|) = 0.2554\) in R2 and \(0.1842\) in R1) and the
alignment of \(\nabla\tilde h_r\) with \(\Delta_\perp\) (median cosine \(0.2113\) and \(0.2817\)).
\item Hold shift: \(0.07439\) (\(0.001438\)) and \(0.09728\) (\(0.0226\)), the
smallest of the four fractions in both regimes. Two constants of \(L_{\mathrm{hold}}\) are conservative here:
\(\|z - z_k\|_2\) reaches at most \(0.3205\) (R2) and \(0.5853\) (R1) of \(M_z s\), and the hold shift
at most \(0.2323\) (R2) and \(0.1794\) (R1) of
\(L_{\mathrm{hold}}\|z - z_k\|_2/M_z\), its Lipschitz bound in \(z\).
\end{itemize}
The realised rate \(\dot{\tilde h}_r + \gamma_0\tilde h_r\) minus the right-hand side of~\eqref{eq:ode_diff_ineq} is
positive at every active-shell node, so the soundness check passes. Its minimum is \(0.6597\,\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\)
in R2 and \(11.5\,\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\) in R1, attained at a sampling instant, where the hold term
vanishes; the Teacher margin at \(z_k\) (the left-hand side of~\eqref{eq:teacher_cbf} minus \(\delta_{\mathrm{total}}\))
supplies a fraction \(0.5084\) (R2) and \(0.9691\) (R1) of it.

\subsection{Experiment 7: Corollary~\ref{cor:stochastic_w2} and ablation (f)}
In R1 the paths start on the level \(0.9\) of \(\tilde{\mathcal C}_r\) (\(h(x_0) = 0.005051\)), with spatially smooth
noise. The intensity is chosen by a grid search over \(\sigma \in \{0.002, 0.003, 0.005, 0.007, 0.01, 0.014, 0.02\}\) (Table~\ref{tab:exp7_sigma_grid}).
\(\sigma^\star = 0.007\) is the largest grid value for which the Corollary accepts at \(T = 2\) and
\(\max_t|h(x_t) - h(x_0)| \ge 0.1\,h(x_0)\) holds on at least half of the paths; the Corollary refuses from
\(\sigma = 0.01\) on (failing condition: margin). On the screening paths at \(\sigma^\star\) the criterion is driven mostly by the closed-loop drift (median
deviation \(8.41\,h(x_0)\)). The noise itself is not negligible: the median deviation from the noise-free
trajectory is \(0.174\,h(x_0)\) (Table~\ref{tab:exp7_sigma_grid}).

Out of 2000 Monte Carlo paths, 0 exit. The exact Clopper--Pearson \(95\%\) upper limit is
\(0.001843 \le \alpha = 0.05\) (Fig.~\ref{fig:exp7}, Table~\ref{tab:exp7_stochastic}). The bound is very
conservative: \(T_{\mathrm{TTL}} = 2022\) exceeds the horizon by a factor of \(1011\), and no exit is
observed.

Ablation (f) uses spatially white small-scale noise. The full Corollary refuses it: the tail envelope
\(\Delta_\sigma = 1.1\) exceeds \(\sqrt{E_{\mathrm{safe}}} = 0.2236\). A cruder bound that ignores the tail
diffusion (\(\bar\sigma_\perp := 0\)) accepts it, yet 2000 of 2000 paths exit (Clopper--Pearson lower
limit \(0.9982 > \alpha\)). The tail-diffusion term is therefore load-bearing. The constants are split into
Tables~\ref{tab:exp7_corollary_det} and~\ref{tab:exp7_corollary_stoch}.

The Corollary is not exercised on R2. There the pointwise penalty \(\gamma_0h_{\mathrm{act}} = 0.007237\)
already exceeds the margin \(\delta_{\mathrm{cert}} - \delta_{\mathrm{total}} = 9.994\times10^{-4}\), so its authority condition
fails even at \(\sigma = 0\) (asserted; Table~\ref{tab:exp1_constants_b}). All stochastic runs use \(r_{W_{\scriptstyle 2}} = 0\),
i.e.\ no distributional drift of the adversary.

\subsection{Experiment 8: numerical method}
At the convergence-test step, explicit Euler is unstable (amplification \(3.73\)). The stiffness is moderate
(spectral radius \(72\), stiffness ratio \(43.83\)). Step halving gives error ratios of
\(4.002\)--\(4.045\) for ETD2RK, i.e.\ order 2 (Fig.~\ref{fig:exp8}, Table~\ref{tab:exp8_convergence}).
The simulator matches the closed-form augmented exponential of a latched linear system to \(2.808\times10^{-14}\), whereas
rounding the latch instants to a step grid gives \(0.01675\). The sparse modal propagation equals a dense nodal
ETD2RK to \(1.928\times10^{-14}\), and two runs give identical SHA-256 hashes (Table~\ref{tab:exp8_numerics}).
